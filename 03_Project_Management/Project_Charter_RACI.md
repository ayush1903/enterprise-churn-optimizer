# Project Charter & RACI Matrix

**Project Name:** Enterprise AI-Augmented Churn Reduction & Retention System  
**Project Lead:** Project Manager  
**Executive Sponsor:** Head of Customer Success / VP of Growth  

---

## 1. Project Purpose & Scope
This project delivers an end-to-end churn detection, analytics dashboard, and automated retention playbook system. It transitions customer operations from a reactive loss-mitigation model to a proactive, predictive retention strategy to protect recurring revenue.

## 3. RACI Matrix (Responsibility Assignment)

* **R (Responsible):** The role that performs the activity.
* **A (Accountable):** The sole decision-maker with final approval.
* **C (Consulted):** Subject matter expert providing vital input.
* **I (Informed):** Kept updated on progress and outcomes.

| Project Activity / Deliverable | Project Manager | Business Analyst | Data Analyst | Customer Success Lead | Exec Sponsor |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **BRD & Requirements Gathering** | A | R | C | C | I |
| **Process Modeling (As-Is / To-Be)** | A | R | I | C | I |
| **ETL Pipeline & Data Cleaning** | A | C | R | I | I |
| **Risk Scoring & SQL Analytics** | A | C | R | I | I |
| **Streamlit Dashboard Development** | A | C | R | C | I |
| **Agile Backlog & Sprint Tracking** | R / A | C | C | I | I |
| **Risk Register & Mitigation Plan** | R / A | C | C | C | I |
| **Final Release & Business Sign-off** | R | C | C | A | A |
